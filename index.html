Original animated series, built frame by frame — stories that hunt in the dark.

REINA Studio is an independent animation studio developing original serialized animated content for a global online audience. Our flagship production, REINA: Queen of Shadows, is currently in production.

The Studio

Founded in 2025, REINA Studio is a small, remote-first production team creating stylized 2D animated series with a cinematic dark-fantasy identity. We run a modern, AI-assisted production pipeline — from storyboarding and character design to final edit and sound — which lets a compact team deliver broadcast-grade episodes on an independent budget. Our first series is rolling out on YouTube, with vertical short-form editions of every episode.

Flagship Series — REINA: Queen of Shadows

In the rain-drowned future city of Nocturne, shadow rifts tear open every night at exactly 2:22. Reina — a huntress with a mechanical arm, twin curved blades, and a living shadow-wolf bound to her — hunts what crawls out of them. A dark action-fantasy serial about the price of every summon: each time her shadow fights for her, it takes a memory in payment.

Watch the series on YouTube: @ReinaQueenOfShadows

  • Format: Serialized episodes, 16:9, ~3–5 minutes
  • Short-form: Two vertical Shorts per episode
  • Genre: Dark action / fantasy animation
  • Status: Season 1 in production
  • Distribution: YouTube, global
